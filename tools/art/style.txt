Illustration for a card in a medieval strategy board game called Dueling Dukes.
Style: hand-painted gouache in the spirit of a 15th-century illuminated manuscript miniature, as reimagined for modern board game art. Warm earthy palette of ochre, russet, moss green and deep blue, with small touches of gold leaf. Soft daylight, gentle shadows, clear silhouettes and a single clear focal point, so it still reads when printed small.
Setting: a small, prosperous late-medieval kingdom of green fields, forested hills, stone towns and mountains with ore mines; people in 14th–15th-century European dress.
Composition: landscape 4:3, full-bleed scene. Do not include any text, letters, numbers, captions, banners with writing, borders, frames, card outlines or game icons.
